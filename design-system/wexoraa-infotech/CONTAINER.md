# Wexoraa Offset container

The user selected option 01 / Offset: a broad rounded container with a raised right shoulder. It replaces the earlier W-notch proposal. Keep the official logo unchanged.

`src/brand/signature.ts` is the normalized geometry source. Run `node scripts/export-brand-frame.mjs` to regenerate the three SVG masters in `public/brand/`. The global decorative SVG clip definition and `.signature-frame` share the same path.

Apply to selected hero, film and project surfaces. Reserve at least the upper 12% of the left side as empty space; text and interactive elements belong below the shoulder. Keep ordinary service illustrations, buttons and forms rectangular. Do not clip sticky ancestors. On mobile reserve additional vertical space above hero and film headings. Project image labels sit below the shoulder.

White is the main canvas, #86C232 is the interactive survey chapter and accent, and #222629 provides dark feature panels and readable type. Supporting colours remain #61892F / #474B4F / #6B6E70. Green text on white is not suitable for small body copy; use charcoal. Keep white/charcoal button hover contrast immediate.
