# Visual assets

hero-art.webp and brand-art.webp are original AI-generated editorial concepts created for this website redesign. They are not photographs of commissioned Wexoraa client projects. The work section labels them as studio concepts.

The generated source images were compressed to WebP without changing their composition. Hero is approximately 155KB; brand concept is approximately 192KB. next/image serves responsive variants. Font families are distributed under their included SIL Open Font License notices.

brand/wexoraa-logo.svg is the user-supplied official SVG, copied byte-for-byte. Its original black, #83C040 and #53B900 fills and 296:60 proportions are preserved. Header/footer render the original file; it is not recreated or recolored.

motion/wexoraa-studio.mp4 is an original silent motion graphic composed from code, the supplied official logo and the licensed Space Grotesk font. It is not client footage. motion/studio-poster.webp is its rendered poster. Editable rendering source is scripts/render-studio-film.mjs. No stock-video or external video-hosting dependency is used.

## Client logo strip

`clients/client-logos.png` is a transparent 2172×724 sprite produced with the image-editing tool from the available inline user-supplied client marks: Intercity Riders, Oneway Drop Taxi, TaerG Laundry and Vlasy Unisex Salon. The repeated Laundry upload is included once in the unique client data. The cream exterior of the salon asset was removed; white lettering inside taxi marks is preserved and displayed on charcoal plates. CSS reveals each quarter without stretching its aspect ratio. These are raster edited renditions, not original vector files or guaranteed pixel-identical exports.

The earlier four inline marks (Exploring Munnar, the Covai home-services mark, Gym Hack and Purple Drop Taxi) were not available as local source files or within the image tool's reference window, so they have not been substituted or fabricated. Current marquee has four unique clients, with decorative repetitions for seamless scrolling. This is based on the user's identification of these businesses as clients; no extra testimonial, result or partnership claim was added.
