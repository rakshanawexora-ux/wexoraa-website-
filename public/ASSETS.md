# Visual assets

hero-art.webp and brand-art.webp are original AI-generated editorial concepts created for this website redesign. They are not photographs of commissioned Wexoraa client projects. The work section labels them as studio concepts.

The generated source images were compressed to WebP without changing their composition. Hero is approximately 155KB; brand concept is approximately 192KB. next/image serves responsive variants. Font families are distributed under their included SIL Open Font License notices.
